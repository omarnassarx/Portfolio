* **Tools:** Power BI Desktop, Excel, DAX, Power Query, Data Cleaning, Data Modelling

### 🛒 Sales Performance Dashboard
* **Tools:** Power BI, Excel, DAX, Power Query, Data Modelling
* **Core Work:** Built an interactive sales dashboard covering 4 years of data (2015-2018). It has five KPI cards (Total Sales, Total Orders, Total Customers, Average Order Value, Average Sales per Customer), Region and Year slicers, and visuals for sales by sub-category, year, region, month (compared across years) and top 10 states. I also wrote up five business insights with recommended actions.
* **Key Findings:**
  * Total sales of 2.26M across 5K orders and 793 customers, with an average order value of 459.42
  * The West region leads with 710K in sales, while the South is lowest at 389K
  * 2018 was the strongest year at 722K, up 20% on 2017
  * Sales consistently spike in March, September and November, pointing to seasonality
  * Phones, Chairs and Binders make up 41% of sales, and Technology is the top category at 43%
* **Business Value:** Highlights where and when revenue is concentrated, so the business can focus marketing on peak months, learn from the West's performance and investigate why Technology leads. I also listed the extra data needed for deeper analysis (product prices, customer demographics, branch data).

### 🏦 Bank Loan Report Dashboard
* **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Data Cleaning
* **Data:** Loan dataset from my data analytics diploma.
* **Core Work:** Cleaned the data in Power Query, built a data model with relationships, and wrote 30 DAX measures covering core aggregations, advanced logic and time intelligence (MTD and MoM). The result is a 3-page interactive report (Summary, Overview, Details) with page navigation buttons and State, Grade and Good vs Bad Loan slicers.
* **What's Inside:**
  * **Summary:** KPI cards for Total Loan Applications, Funded Amount, Amount Received, Average Interest Rate and Average DTI, each with MTD and MoM comparisons. It also has a Good vs Bad loan split and a Loan Status breakdown table.
  * **Overview:** Applications by month, by state (map), by loan term, by employment length, by loan purpose and by home ownership.
  * **Details:** A loan-level table with purpose, home ownership, grade and sub-grade, issue date, funded amount, interest rate, installment and received amount.
* **Key Findings:**
  * About 38.6K loan applications, with $436M funded and $473M received
  * Average interest rate of 12.0% and average DTI of 13.3%
  * 86.2% of applications are good loans (Fully Paid and Current) and 13.8% are bad (Charged Off)
  * Debt consolidation is the top loan purpose at 18.2K applications, and 73.2% of loans are on 36-month terms
  * Applications grew steadily through the year, from 2.3K in January to 4.3K in December
* **Business Value:** Lets lending and risk teams monitor portfolio health, loan performance and risk indicators in one place, and slice by state, grade or loan quality to see where bad loans concentrate.
