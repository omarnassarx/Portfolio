## 📁 Featured Projects

### 🏦 Client Climate Risk Portfolio Dashboard
* **Tools:** Power BI Desktop, Excel
* **Context:** Built in my current role in Bank NXT's Sustainability Department for senior management reporting.
* **Core Work:** Built a Power BI dashboard on an excel extract of business banking clients (governorate, industry, detailed sector and direct facility). It has a governorate slicer that drives an industry matrix showing client count, share of clients, direct facility amount and share of facilities, with a grand total row. A few KPI cards and a governorate comparison shape map sit alongside it, designed to be clean and executive-friendly.
* **Quality & Maintenance:** Reconciled the dashboard totals against the Risk department's figures (they matched) and refreshed it consistently.
* **Business Value:** Gave management a clear view of how the client base and facility exposure are distributed across governorates and industries, and the insights supported decisions by the Sustainability Director.
* 🔒 **Confidential:** This project uses internal bank data, so no screenshots, data or links can be shared publicly.

### 🛒 Sales Performance Dashboard
* **Tools:** Power BI
* **Core Work:** Built an interactive sales dashboard covering 4 years of data (2015-2018). It has five KPI cards (Total Sales, Total Orders, Total Customers, Average Order Value, Average Sales per Customer), Region and Year slicers, and visuals for sales by sub-category, year, region, month (compared across years) and top 10 states. I also wrote up five business insights with recommended actions.
* **Key Findings:**
  * Total sales of 2.26M across 5K orders and 793 customers, with an average order value of 459.42
  * The West region leads with 710K in sales, while the South is lowest at 389K
  * 2018 was the strongest year at 722K, up 20% on 2017
  * Sales consistently spike in March, September and November, pointing to seasonality
  * Phones, Chairs and Binders make up 41% of sales, and Technology is the top category at 43%
* **Business Value:** Highlights where and when revenue is concentrated, so the business can focus marketing on peak months, learn from the West's performance and investigate why Technology leads. I also listed the extra data needed for deeper analysis (product prices, customer demographics, branch data).
